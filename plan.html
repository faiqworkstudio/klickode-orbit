01The idea

Sell the flight, not the website.

Most small businesses have a website that looks like every other website. A few big brands have homepages that feel like a short film, but those take agencies months and cost far more than a shop, café or startup can pay.

Orbit closes that gap. We already have the engine: a 3D space you scroll through, a planet in your colours, chapters of real text, and a logo that arrives at the end. Each new client is a new world on the same engine, so the tenth build costs a fraction of the first.

The design is the marketing. People screenshot it, record it and share it. Every Orbit we ship carries a small “Made in Orbit” link, so every client site becomes an ad.

Orbit Kit

Buy the engine

Template source code for designers, developers and makers worldwide. Sold online, delivered instantly, no time cost per sale.

Orbit Launch

We build your world

A custom flight for a product launch, opening or campaign, live in 14 days. Highest price, and the work that fills the showcase.

Orbit Care

Keep it alive

Hosting, edits and seasonal worlds every month. Small, but it repeats, and it keeps clients coming back for the next launch.

02Why it sells

The market is tired of templates.

Attention

Feeds reward motion

TikTok, Reels and Shorts favour things that move. A 20-second screen recording of an Orbit flight is native content for those feeds; a static homepage is not.

Sameness

AI made sites cheap and alike

Website builders and AI tools now produce a decent page in minutes. That pushes the value toward what they cannot produce on demand: a distinct, crafted experience.

Proven demand

People already buy templates

Designers and developers routinely pay for premium templates on Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Framer and Webflow marketplaces. Real-time 3D templates are still rare there.

Home advantage

Thai brands launch all year

Bangkok has a constant flow of new cafés, beauty brands, condos, events and festivals, each needing a launch page. We work in Thai and English at Bangkok prices.

06Marketing

Let the work do the shouting.

Orbit is visual, so the plan leans on showing it moving. The rule: every week, publish one new flight, as a video clip, a live demo, or a client site.

Fly-through videos

Record 15–25 second scroll-throughs of each world, vertical, with sound. Post to TikTok, Reels, Shorts and X. Caption with the brand and “made in a browser”.

Free · 2 h/wk

08Risks

What could go wrong, and the answer.

RiskWhat we do about it
Slow on cheap phonesAlready lower particle counts on small screens. Add a frame-rate check that drops quality automatically, and test on a ฿4,000 Android phone before every release.
Someone copies the lookThe engine is easy to copy, the service is not. Keep shipping new worlds faster than copies appear, and sell the kit with updates so buyers stay with the original.
3D starts to feel like a gimmickEvery chapter carries a real message and real text. Sell Orbit on results (time on page, shares), not effects.
Launch work crowds out everything elseCap at 3 Launches a month until there is a second builder. Raise the price before taking more.
Kit buyers need supportOne settings file, a clear guide, and a short video for each step. Answer support by email twice a week.
