01Orbit’s job

The flagship that makes the whole studio look bigger.

03Name system

Speak Orbit everywhere.

A name sticks when every touchpoint repeats it. These words go on the site, in quotes, in invoices and in the videos, so a client lives the idea from first click to final payment.

Mission
Every project, with a code: ORB-001, ORB-002. The code goes on the quote, the invoice and the site footer.
World
The planet theme a brand flies in: Klickode Red, Nebula Violet, Andaman Blue, Saffron Gold, Jade Garden, or a custom one.
Lift-off date
Launches go live on the 1st, 10th or 20th. Three windows a month makes the calendar visible and the scarcity real.
Flight
The scroll experience itself. “Fly through” replaces “scroll down” in every call to action.
Mission log
The portfolio. Each entry shows the brief, the idea, the chapters, the video and the results.
Stay in orbit
The Care plan. Leaving Care is “landing”, and landed clients can relaunch any time.
Gravity
The one offer on each ring that pulls a customer inward. Used internally to design every offer.
Fly-through
The 20-second video of each flight. The main piece of marketing, made for every mission.

05Market

Why now, and for whom.

Attention

Feeds reward motion

Short-video feeds favour things that move. A fly-through is native content there; a static homepage is not.

Sameness

AI made websites cheap and alike

Builders and AI tools produce a decent page in minutes. That moves the value toward what they cannot make on demand: a crafted, memorable experience.

Proven demand

People already buy templates

Designers pay for premium templates on Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Framer and Webflow. Real-time 3D templates are still rare there.

Home advantage

Bangkok launches all year

New cafés, beauty brands, condos, resorts and festivals each need a launch page. Klickode works in Thai and English at Bangkok prices.

09Risks

What could go wrong, and the answer.

RiskWhat we do about it
Slow on cheap phonesFewer particles on small screens already. Add an automatic quality drop when the frame rate falls, and test on a ฿4,000 Android phone before every lift-off.
Someone copies the lookThe engine can be copied; the mission system, the log and the service cannot. Keep shipping new worlds faster than copies appear.
3D starts to feel like a gimmickEvery chapter carries a real message in real text. Report results (time on page, shares, sales), not effects.
Orbit pulls the studio off courseThree lift-off windows a month is a hard cap. Studio work stays the core; Orbit is the gravity around it.
Kit support eats timeOne settings file, a clear guide, a short video per step. Support by email twice a week.
