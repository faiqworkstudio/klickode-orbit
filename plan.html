01The product

A website, a directory listing and a show.

Each company gets a numbered planet in the Genesis galaxy. Visitors who open it warp in and land in a world themed in the company’s colours: hero, numbers, story, services, a quote and contacts. The company edits it any time from the builder.

For the company

One link that impresses

A mini-site that feels like a launch, ready in ten minutes. Perfect for small businesses that have only a Facebook page or a stale website.

For visitors

A galaxy worth exploring

Search by industry or city, tap lights on a map, fly into companies. Discovery feels like play, so people browse longer.

For Klickode

Recurring revenue and leads

Monthly plans pay the bills. Flagship builds and the clients they lead to grow the studio.

09Risks

What could go wrong, and the answer.

RiskWhat we do about it
Companies cancel after the noveltyMonthly visitor reports show value; yearly billing with two months free; seasonal sky themes keep worlds fresh.
Fake or misleading companiesCheck business registration before a world goes public; report button on every world; certificates only for checked companies.
Slow on cheap phonesWorlds use a light 2D sky, not full 3D. The heavy 3D stays on the galaxy home page.
Empty galaxy at launchKlickode clients move in free, Flagship Founders fill numbers 1–99, and clusters make each area feel busy.
Search engines can’t read itEach world also renders as plain HTML at its own link, with title and description from the builder.
