01The product

What people actually buy.

Not a website and not a picture. A buyer gets a place in a universe other people can visit. That is the difference between Orbit and a printable “name a star” certificate: the planet exists, it has neighbours, and anyone can search it and step inside.

Own

A number no one else has

One of 9,999 Genesis planets, locked to one owner, with a certificate and a fingerprint code. NFT-style scarcity with no wallet and no crypto.

Create

A planet that looks like them

Colours, ring, moon, crystals and a name. Then a world inside: a dedication and three short chapters.

Belong

A place others can find

A shared galaxy map, a search box and a link per planet. Owners show it off; visitors become the next buyers.

02Why it sells

Four reasons people say yes.

Gift

A proven gift idea, made better

Naming a star has been a popular gift for decades. Orbit keeps the feeling and fixes the weak part: you can actually see, visit and share what you bought.

Collectible

NFT excitement, none of the pain

People liked owning a numbered, one-of-a-kind thing. They did not like wallets, gas fees and scams. Orbit pays in baht with PromptPay or card.

Culture

Thailand pays for lucky numbers

Lucky phone numbers and licence plates already sell for extra. Founders numbers, 888 and 9999 turn that habit into the highest-margin item in the shop.

Share

Every planet is content

A reveal video (“I bought you a planet”), a link, a certificate photo. Buyers post it because it is about someone they love.

09Risks

Keep it honest and safe.

RiskWhat we do about it
People think it is a real planetSay it plainly on the page, at checkout and on the certificate: a planet in the Klickode Orbit universe, not an astronomical body.
It looks like an investmentNever promise value or resale profit. Transfers are for gifting. No crypto, no tokens, prices in baht.
Rude or hurtful namesWord filter at claim, a report button on every planet, and a moderation queue checked daily. Removed planets are refunded.
Losing the registryDaily backups and an ownership log. The certificate fingerprint lets any owner prove their planet’s original details.
“Name a star” copycatsCopy the certificate and you still lack the universe: search, neighbours, the world inside and the 3D flight. Keep adding to those.
Refunds and disputesFull refund within 7 days if the planet link has not been shared. Clear terms at checkout.
