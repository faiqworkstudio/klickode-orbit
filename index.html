Does this replace my website?

For many small businesses, yes: one link with your story, services and contacts. If you need a shop or booking system, Klickode Studio builds that and links it from your world.

What happens to my planet if I stop paying?

Your world goes dark but your number is held for 60 days. Pay again and it lights up exactly as you left it.

Is this a real planet?

No. It is a planet in the Klickode Orbit universe, a digital world made by Klickode Studio. Your company owns its registry entry and everything inside it.

Can I print the business card?

Yes. The share kit saves the front and back at 3.5 × 2 inches and 300 dpi, the standard size printers expect. The back carries a QR code that opens your world.

Do you work with bigger brands?

Yes. Brand systems give a company several planets (one per brand, branch or campaign), a product modelled in 3D, monthly reporting and a named contact at Klickode Studio. Book a demo for a quote.

Can Klickode build it for me?

Yes. Flagship includes design, copy in Thai and English, a 3D version of your logo or product, and a short fly-in video for social media.
