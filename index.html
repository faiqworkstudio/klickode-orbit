Step 3 · Keep

Proof it’s yours.

Every planet comes with a numbered certificate of ownership. This one is drawn live from the planet you are designing.

  • One number, one owner. The registry refuses a number that is already taken, so your planet can never be sold twice.
  • A fingerprint, like an NFT. Each certificate carries a code made from the planet’s details. Change one detail and the code changes.
  • A seal no one else has. The pattern on the seal is drawn from that fingerprint, so every certificate looks different.
  • Easy to check. Anyone can search the number and see the same planet, name and owner.

Is this a real planet?

No. It is a planet in the Klickode Orbit universe, a digital world made by Klickode Studio. You own its registry entry, its design and the world inside it. It is a keepsake, not an astronomical body or an investment.

Is it an NFT?

It works like one, without the hard parts. Every planet has a unique number, a single owner and a fingerprint code. You do not need a crypto wallet, and you pay in baht.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. Put their name in “Registered to”, write the dedication, and send them the link or the printed certificate.

What are lucky numbers?

Some numbers are special: the first 99 planets, repeating digits like 888 or 9999, and numbers ending in 9 or 8. They cost a little more and show in gold on the map.

Can I change my planet later?

The name, number and owner are fixed once claimed, so the certificate stays true. Changing the world inside will be a paid edit at launch.
